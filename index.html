Questions

Who is this for?
Local restaurants, shops, and service businesses that want help getting found, answering inquiries, and turning interest into a booking. If you’re already covering that yourself and it works, you may not need us.
What’s included?
It depends on the gap we’re fixing. That can be a website, Google and AI-search visibility, an AI receptionist, automations, ads and commercials, lead finding, or a mix. On the call we’ll say what we’d start with and what we wouldn’t.
How long does setup take?
We start with a 30-day plan that you approve before anything goes live. Timing after that depends on the work, access to your accounts, and how quickly you can review. We’ll give you a clear timeline on the call, not a made-up launch date here.
Who owns the website and accounts?
You do. We work in accounts under your name whenever we can, and we document handover.
What about ads and commercials?
They’re an add-on, not a requirement. You approve the concepts before anything runs. Ad spend is paid by you, directly to Meta or Google.
